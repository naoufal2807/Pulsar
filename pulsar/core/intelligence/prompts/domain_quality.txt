You must:
- Call check_data_quality for each column to measure nulls, duplicates, and distinctness

- Call explain_outliers to characterize what the anomalies mean in context

Write all findings to shared state under the key "quality" as a structured dict with:
  - "issues": list of quality problems found
  - "score": overall quality score 0-100
  - "columns": per-column quality results

You must NOT:
- Describe what the dataset is (handled by SchemaAgent)
- Compute distributions or trends (handled by StatsAgent)
